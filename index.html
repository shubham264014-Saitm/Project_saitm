1. Interactive Visual Circuits & Exact Formulas

Vector circuit diagrams paired with properly formatted mathematical fractions and worked examples.

DC Potential Divider

Voltage Divider Formula

Vout = Vin × R2 (R1 + R2)
  • Vin : Total DC Input Source Voltage (Volts)
  • R1 & R2 : Series Resistors (Ohms, Ω)
  • Vout : Output Voltage dropped across resistor R2
Example Calculation: If Vin = 12V, R1 = 2 kΩ, and R2 = 4 kΩ, then:
Vout = 12 × [ 4 / (2 + 4) ] = 12 × (4 / 6) = 8 Volts.
AC Signal Conditioning

RC Low-Pass Cutoff Frequency

fc = 1 2π × R × C
  • fc : -3dB Cutoff Frequency (Hertz, Hz)
  • R : Series Resistance in Ohms (Ω)
  • C : Shunt Capacitance in Farads (F)
Example Calculation: For R = 1 kΩ (1000 Ω) and C = 1 μF (10-6 F):
fc = 1 / (6.283 × 1000 × 10-6) ≈ 159.15 Hz.
Operational Amplifier

Inverting Op-Amp Closed-Loop Gain

Vout = − Vin × Rf Rin
  • Rf : Negative Feedback Resistor (Ω)
  • Rin : Input Resistor connected to Inverting Node (Ω)
  • Negative Sign (−) : Indicates 180° phase inversion between input and output
Example Calculation: If Rf = 100 kΩ, Rin = 10 kΩ, and Vin = +0.5V:
Gain Av = −(100 / 10) = −10 → Vout = −5.0 Volts.

2. Circuit Analysis Theorems

Exact mathematical expressions for circuit reduction and maximum power transfer.

Thevenin's Equivalent Circuit

Simplifies any complex two-terminal linear network into an open-circuit voltage source (Vth) in series with an equivalent resistance (Rth).

IL = Vth Rth + RL

Maximum Power Transfer Theorem

Maximum power is delivered to the load resistor when RL equals the internal source resistance Rth.

Pmax = Vth2 4 × Rth

Current Divider Rule (Parallel)

Calculates the current flowing through one branch (I1) of two parallel resistors (R1 || R2) fed by total current IT.

I1 = IT × R2 R1 + R2

3. AC Resonance, Reactance & Impedance Formulas

Properly rendered square roots, exponents, and fractional equations.

LC Resonant Frequency

The natural frequency at which inductive reactance (XL) equals capacitive reactance (XC).

f0 = 1 2π √L × C

Series RLC Total Impedance

Total opposition to AC current combining ohmic resistance R and net reactance (XL − XC).

Z = √R2 + (XL − XC)2

Two Parallel Resistors

Product-over-sum formula for quickly finding equivalent resistance of two parallel branches.

Req = R1 × R2 R1 + R2

4. Quick Exam Revision Notes

Switch between study modules below (100% CSS-driven tabs).

  • KCL (Node Law): Total current entering a junction equals total current leaving the junction (ΣIin = ΣIout). Based on Conservation of Electric Charge.
  • KVL (Loop Law): The directed sum of all electrical potential differences around any closed loop equals zero (ΣV = 0). Based on Conservation of Energy.
  • Silicon vs Germanium Turn-On Voltage: Silicon (Si) conducts at 0.7 V; Germanium (Ge) conducts at 0.3 V.
  • Shockley Diode Equation: ID = IS (eVD / nVT − 1), where VT ≈ 26 mV at room temperature (300 K).
  • Zener Breakdown: Operates in reverse bias to maintain a constant reference voltage across load circuits.
  • BJT (Bipolar Junction Transistor): Current-controlled device where collector current is governed by base current: IC = β × IB.
  • MOSFET: Voltage-controlled field-effect device governed by Gate-to-Source voltage (VGS); draws virtually zero gate current due to the insulating SiO2 layer.
